Tax treatment: the AU market on autorepaircost.net documents prices as GST-inclusive. Ranges are nationwide, before state labour adjustment. In 35 rows (2.9%) the labour+parts bounds do not sum exactly to the total bounds — use total_* as authoritative.

Provenance & uncertainty — read before relying on a row

  • 175 rows (14.6%) are URL-anchored to a real AutoGuru page: prefix the source value with https://, open it, and the quoted range should bracket our total_min–total_max.
  • 1,007 rows (83.9%) are model-estimated: a documented generic baseline × per-brand factor method, reproducible — not hand-invented.
  • 18 rows (1.5%) are curated from published Australian sources.
  • 98.2% of rows are graded medium: indicative market ranges, not workshop quotes. Verify high-stakes figures against the anchored source page before relying on a single row.
